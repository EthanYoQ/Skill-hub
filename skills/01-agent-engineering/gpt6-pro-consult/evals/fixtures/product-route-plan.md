# Team Workflow Product Route

## Target

Help small product teams turn meeting notes into assigned tasks and weekly status summaries.

## Proposed route

- Build a broad workspace product with notes, tasks, chat, calendar sync, and dashboards.
- Offer a free tier immediately and monetize advanced analytics later.
- Use weekly active workspaces as the primary success metric.
- Launch to design agencies first, then expand to software teams.

## Assumptions

- Teams will replace existing notes and task tools rather than use an integration.
- More modules will improve retention.
- Agencies and software teams have similar approval and security needs.
- A free tier will create enough usage data to discover willingness to pay.

## Constraints

- Four-person engineering team.
- Twelve-week runway for a credible paid pilot.
- No existing calendar or task-management integrations.

## Decision needed

Choose between the broad workspace route and a narrow meeting-to-accountability workflow that integrates with existing tools.
